# Shopify Payment Setup Guide

## Current Status
✅ **Razorpay** (UPI, Cards, Wallets, Net Banking) — **Active**

Your store payments are configured. Customers can pay via Razorpay when they complete checkout in the app.

---

## How It Works
1. Customer adds items to cart in the app
2. Taps "Proceed to Payment" → navigates to Shopify checkout
3. Shopify checkout shows Razorpay options (UPI, Cards, Wallets, NB)
4. Payment is processed by Razorpay through Shopify

---

## Managing Payment Methods
- **Shopify Admin** → Settings → Payments
- Add more providers: Third-party providers → Add provider
- Enable/disable: Toggle each provider in the list

---

## Manual Payments (Optional)
You can also add:
- **Cash on Delivery** — Settings → Payments → Manual payments
- **Bank Transfer** — Settings → Payments → Manual payments
